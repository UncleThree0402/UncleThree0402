## About me

I moved from platform engineering into a software engineering role in a product team. I now work closer to the product, but I still find myself thinking about the infrastructure underneath it and the tools we use to build it.

I enjoy making things work. I also enjoy asking why getting them to work involved so many steps in the first place. That curiosity tends to turn into automation, a small tool or another experiment in my lab.

## Things I keep coming back to

**Developer tooling**

I'm interested in making everyday development smoother. Repetitive tasks, confusing feedback and awkward workflows are all good reasons to build something useful. A tool doesn't have to be impressive to be worth making. Saving someone a daily frustration is enough.

**AI agents, harnesses and governance**

I'm curious about what agents can do and what it takes to work with them properly. How do we give them useful context? How do we guide their actions? How do we make sense of what happened when something goes wrong? Those questions interest me as much as building the agent itself.

**Platforms and infrastructure**

Platform engineering still feels like home. I enjoy working on systems that can be understood, rebuilt and maintained through code. My lab is where I explore those ideas and find out which ones survive contact with a running system.

**Go**

Go has caught my interest. I'd like to explore it by building tools I'd actually use.
